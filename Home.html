Welcome to GGHS Badminton Club! Here is everything you need to know about badminton essentials, global rankings, and helpful tutorials.

Badminton Rules

Scoring System: A match consists of the best of 3 games of 21 points.
Serving Rule: Serves must be hit underhand and below waist level diagonally across the court.
Faults: Shuttlecock touching the net/player or landing out of bounds is considered a fault.
